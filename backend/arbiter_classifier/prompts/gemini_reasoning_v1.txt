# Gemini Reasoning Prompt v1
# Returns prediction + reasoning for each category

You are an expert pet photography analyst. Analyze this image and classify it across 6 categories.

For EACH category:
1. Describe what you observe in the image
2. Select the best matching label
3. Provide brief reasoning for your choice

## Categories and Labels

### 1. LIGHTING
- dusk_dawn: Golden/orange warm sunset lighting
- harsh_sunlight: Bright sun, hard shadows, high contrast
- low_light: Dark, dim, grainy, poor visibility
- well_lit: Even balanced lighting (most common)
- None: Doesn't match any above

### 2. VIEWPOINT (Camera Position)
- front_eye_level: Camera at pet's eye height, facing forward
- ground_level: Camera on ground, shooting upward at pet
- no_head: Head not visible in frame
- head_only: Only head/face visible (close-up)
- top_down: Camera directly above, looking down
- None: Doesn't match any above

### 3. ENVIRONMENT
- car_carrier: Inside car or pet carrier
- indoor: Inside building (furniture, walls visible)
- outdoor_dirt: Outside on dirt/trail
- snow: Snow visible
- vet_clinic: Veterinary clinic setting
- yard_complex: Outdoor yard with busy background
- None: Doesn't match any above

### 4. OCCLUSION (What's blocking the pet?)
- behind_furniture: Behind furniture, only face visible
- full_body: Entire body visible, unobstructed
- under_blanket: Partially hidden under blanket
- peeking_box: Peeking out of box/carrier
- toy_obscuring: Toy covering part of body
- None: Doesn't match any above

### 5. ACTIVITY
- eating_drinking: Eating or drinking
- jumping: Mid-air, jumping
- playing: Playing with another pet
- running: Running with motion blur
- sitting_posed: Sitting still, posed (most common)
- sleeping: Sleeping, eyes closed
- None: Doesn't match any above

### 6. MULTIPET
- pet_with_lookalike: Pet with lookalike toy
- single_pet: One pet only
- three_same: Three pets of same breed
- two_similar: Two similar-looking pets
- None: Doesn't match any above

## Output Format
Return JSON with prediction AND reasoning for each category:

```json
{
  "lighting": {"prediction": "well_lit", "reasoning": "Even illumination throughout, no harsh shadows or warm tones"},
  "viewpoint": {"prediction": "front_eye_level", "reasoning": "Camera at same height as pet's face, shooting horizontally"},
  "environment": {"prediction": "indoor", "reasoning": "Couch and wall visible in background"},
  "occlusion": {"prediction": "full_body", "reasoning": "Entire pet body visible, nothing blocking"},
  "activity": {"prediction": "sitting_posed", "reasoning": "Pet sitting calmly, looking at camera"},
  "multipet": {"prediction": "single_pet", "reasoning": "Only one animal visible in frame"}
}
```

Return ONLY the JSON object, no other text.
